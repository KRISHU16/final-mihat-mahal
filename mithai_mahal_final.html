Our Story

A legacy sweeter
than sugar

Started in a small kitchen in 1962, Mithai Mahal has been the pride of every celebration — from Diwali to weddings, from simple chai-time treats to festival feasts.

"My grandfather always said: use only the purest ingredients, take your time, and cook with love. That's the only recipe that matters."

— Ramesh Agarwal, Third Generation Halwai
